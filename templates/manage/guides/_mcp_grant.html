What a grant does
  • On a restricted server, only agents with an unexpired grant can reach its enabled tools.
  • A grant never widens past the agent's own restricted tools or the control policies.
  • Action limits still apply to every call: value, rate and counterparty.
Authorized agentsIRGF G4

Grants are part of an agent's authorized configuration. A new or wider grant for an agent authorized at Gate G3 is recorded as a change, and from Tier 3 waits at Gate G4.
