After registering
  1. Approval for use. A governance decision, on the model's page, for whoever holds model.approve. Unapproved models are refused in production by the default policies, and routing skips them.
  2. Evaluation evidence. If a model-approval policy requires it, run a suite in the Evaluation Centre first.
  3. Routing. Without a routing policy the gateway ranks every active, approved model of the requested type on cost, latency and reliability.
Pricing

Prices are per 1,000 tokens, in the model's currency. Every run's cost is derived from them when an engine does not report one, and versioned price lists can override them for past periods.
