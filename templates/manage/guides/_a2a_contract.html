What a contract does
  • On a restricted agent, only a sender with an unexpired contract gets through, for the skills it names.
  • The ceiling holds what the message may carry. Personal data found in a message makes it at least confidential, whatever was declared.
  • Control policies, the hop limit and both agents' kill-switches still apply to every message.
Authorized agentsIRGF G4

A contract is part of the sender's authorized configuration. A new or wider one for an agent authorized at Gate G3 is recorded as a change, and from Tier 3 waits at Gate G4. Until then, the Control Plane reports traffic along it as outside the agent's authorized boundary.
