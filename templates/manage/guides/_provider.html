After registering
  1. Set the credential. Put the secret in the platform's environment under the variable you name here, for the web processes and the workers alike, then restart them.
  2. Health check. On the provider's page. The registry shows the credential as Resolved once the platform can find it.
  3. Refresh capabilities from the adapter, so routing knows what it can do.
  4. Approval. Someone who holds model.approve approves it for production; the default provider policy requires it.
  5. Register its models with their prices.
Adapters

openai reaches the OpenAI API or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint, generic_rest any JSON-over-HTTP service described by its options, and mock answers without credentials, for demos and tests.

A new vendor is one adapter class, registered in integrations/.
