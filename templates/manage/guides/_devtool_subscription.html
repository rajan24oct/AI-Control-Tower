What is read from GitHub
  • Seats: who holds one, the plan, the GitHub team that granted it and when it was last used (/orgs/{org}/copilot/billing/seats).
  • Premium requests, per person per day, from the organization's billing usage report.
  • Seats map to people by e-mail, or by the login_map option; teams come from the granting GitHub team's slug, or the person's team here. Fix any seat by hand and the mapping is kept.
How it is priced

Each seat's licence, prorated by day, plus premium requests beyond its monthly allowance at the overage rate. Change the rates to match your agreement.

Use the mock_github_copilot integration to try the page with demo data; its seats come from options.seats.
