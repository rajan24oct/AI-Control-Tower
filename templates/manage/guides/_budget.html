How spend is counted
  • Every run's cost, from engine telemetry or served by the AI Gateway, priced from the model registry when the engine reports none.
  • An agent, team, provider or model budget counts only the runs attributable to it; an organization budget counts everything.
  • Thresholds are checked every 15 minutes, and once when you save.
Hard stops

A hard stop acts only where the platform holds the request: AI Gateway calls get 402 budget_exceeded. Traffic outside the gateway can only be alerted on. GATEWAY_ENFORCE_BUDGETS=0 makes every hard stop advisory at once.
