How spend is counted
  • AI runtime: every run's cost, from engine telemetry or served by the AI Gateway, priced from the model registry when the engine reports none.
  • Developer tools: GitHub Copilot seat licences, prorated by day, plus premium requests past each seat's allowance.
  • A department or team gets a run through its application, or its agent if it served none; an individual through the person the run was attributed to, or the Copilot seat they hold.
  • Thresholds are checked every 15 minutes, and once when you save.
Thresholds and notifications

Each threshold alerts once a period. A forecast alert fires once when spend so far projects past the limit, so there is time to act before the money is gone.

Cost managers always hear about budgets; the owner, the team lead and any extra recipients are added as you choose. E-mail follows each person's notification preferences.

Hard stops

A hard stop acts only where the platform holds the request: AI Gateway calls get 402 budget_exceeded. Traffic outside the gateway, and Copilot, can only be alerted on. GATEWAY_ENFORCE_BUDGETS=0 makes every hard stop advisory at once.
