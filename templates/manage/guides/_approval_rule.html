How rules are used
  • Engines ask POST /api/v1/approval-requests/ to raise a request; every rule that matches is recorded on it.
  • The gateways cannot wait for a person, so a call a rule holds is refused with 202 approval_required.
  • Requests expire after 24 hours unless the engine asks for longer.
Tier 3–4IRGF

When a request concerns a Tier 3 or 4 system, neither the person who raised it nor the system's owners may decide it: the decision leaves the delivery line.
