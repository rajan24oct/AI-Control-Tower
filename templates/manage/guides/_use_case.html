What Gate G1 asks
  • A named need, how it is measured today and the target.
  • The non-AI alternative, and why it is not enough.
  • The one decision the output supports, and who it affects.
  • Launch autonomy, a data path with its sensitivity, and provisional D1–D5 scores.
  • No architecture or vendor commitment yet: G1 decides whether to proceed.
Who decides

Tier 1 with internal data at most and no sector constraints is approved by rule.

Tiers 1–2 otherwise: the Risk and Compliance Lead or the AI Governance Body.

Tiers 3–4: the AI Governance Body, not the sponsor. Nobody decides a use case they proposed.
