One per application
  • Every call a key makes is recorded against this account, so give each application or engine its own.
  • Its role is capped below administrator: a leaked key can never hand out access.
  • Keys are issued on the account's page and shown once. Rotate by issuing a new key, deploying it, then revoking the old one.
Binding agentsIRGF link 02

Bound to agents, the account may act only as them. Unbound, it may act for any agent up to Tier 2. A Tier 3–4 agent answers only to identities bound to it, and the gateways refuse any other key with 403 identity_not_bound.
