About organizations

An organization is the tenant boundary. Its agents, applications, policies, budgets and audit trail are invisible to every other organization, and each person's role is set per organization.

    {% if creating %}
  • First administrator. Adds the organization's people, teams and service accounts. It can be you. An organization always keeps at least one.
    • {% endif %}
  • Short name. What scripts and commands such as generate_executions call it. It is fixed once the organization exists.
  • Its people. Add, change or remove anyone from the organization's page, whichever organization is active for you.
  • Its modules. Switch each module, and the IRGF Agent, on or off for this organization. A module that is off leaves the menus and its pages; its data stays, and the API keeps serving.

Only site administrators, who are superusers, see these pages. Creating an organization and every membership change are recorded in that organization's audit trail.
